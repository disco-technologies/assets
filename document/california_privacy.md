# California Privacy Rights

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Effective:** 2026-07-07
**Last Updated:** 2026-07-07

This notice supplements the Disco Privacy Policy for California residents and is provided under the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act ("CCPA/CPRA"). Capitalized terms have the meanings given in the Privacy Policy.

## 1. Categories of Personal Information We Collect

In the preceding 12 months we have collected the following categories of personal information:

| Category                         | Examples on Disco                                                               |
| -------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Identifiers**                  | Name, username, email address, phone number, account ID                            |
| **Profile & content**            | Profile photo, bio, favorite teams, posts, comments, messages                      |
| **Geolocation data**             | Approximate or precise location when you enable location features, venue check-ins |
| **Device information**           | Device type, operating system, app version, device identifiers, IP address         |
| **Internet or network activity** | In-app usage, features used, pages viewed, crash and diagnostic logs               |
| **Inferences**                   | Content and connection suggestions derived from your activity                      |

We collect this information directly from you, from your device (with permission), and from your use of the Service.

## 2. Purposes

We use personal information to operate and personalize the Service; connect you with friends, crews, and venues; keep the community safe (moderation, anti-abuse, security); provide support; and improve the app through analytics and crash diagnostics. Full detail is in the Privacy Policy.

## 3. No Sale of Personal Information

**Disco does not sell your personal information, and does not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising**, as those terms are defined by the CCPA/CPRA. We have not done so in the preceding 12 months. We honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals as an opt-out preference should our practices ever change.

## 4. Your Rights

As a California resident, you have the right to:

- **Know** — request the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you, the sources, purposes, and third parties with whom it is disclosed;
- **Delete** — request deletion of personal information we have collected from you, subject to legal exceptions;
- **Correct** — request correction of inaccurate personal information;
- **Opt out of sale or sharing** — not currently applicable, since we do not sell or share personal information;
- **Limit use of sensitive personal information** — we use precise location only to provide the features you enable, which does not require a separate limit request;
- **Non-discrimination** — we will never deny you the Service, charge you differently, or degrade your experience for exercising any of these rights.

## 5. How to Exercise Your Rights

Email `support@godisco.app` from the email address on your account, or use the in-app tools (Settings → Privacy for data export and account deletion; Settings → Legal → Manage Tracking & Analytics for analytics preferences). We verify requests by matching them to your account and respond within 45 days, extendable once by a further 45 days where reasonably necessary. You may use an authorized agent with signed written permission.

## 6. Retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above and as described in the Privacy Policy's retention section, then delete or de-identify it.
