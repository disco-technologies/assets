# Disco Acceptable Use Policy

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Effective:** 2026-07-07
**Last Updated:** 2026-07-07

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") governs the technical use of the Disco applications, websites, and APIs (the "Service"). It supplements our Terms of Service and Community Guidelines: the Community Guidelines cover what you may say and post; this AUP covers how you may interact with the Service itself. Violating this AUP is grounds for suspension or termination of your account.

## 1. No Unauthorized Access

You may not:

- Access, probe, or test the vulnerability of the Service or any related system or network without written authorization;
- Breach or circumvent any authentication, rate-limiting, or security measure;
- Access another user's account, data, or session, with or without their permission;
- Interfere with service to any user, host, or network, including by overloading, flooding, or mail-bombing.

## 2. No Scraping or Bulk Collection

You may not scrape, crawl, harvest, or otherwise collect data from the Service — including profiles, check-ins, venue data, and messages — by automated means without our prior written consent. Search-engine indexing of publicly available web pages under standard robots directives is permitted.

## 3. No Reverse Engineering

You may not decompile, disassemble, reverse engineer, or otherwise attempt to derive the source code of the apps or the Service, except to the extent this restriction is prohibited by applicable law. You may not remove or alter any proprietary notices.

## 4. API and Client Integrity

The Service's APIs are for use by official Disco clients only. You may not:

- Build or distribute alternative clients, bots, or tools that call our APIs without written authorization;
- Forge headers, spoof device identifiers, or misrepresent the origin of any request;
- Use the Service to develop a competing product, or benchmark it for competitive purposes without consent.

## 5. Account Integrity

You may not create accounts by automated means; register accounts under false identities; buy, sell, rent, or trade accounts; or operate coordinated networks of accounts to amplify content or manipulate rankings, check-in counts, or engagement.

## 6. No Abuse of the Platform

You may not use the Service to send spam or unsolicited bulk messages; distribute malware or malicious links; run phishing or social-engineering campaigns; spoof GPS location or falsify check-ins; or relay traffic to conceal the origin of abusive activity.

## 7. Security Research

We welcome responsible disclosure. If you discover a vulnerability, report it to `support@godisco.app` and do not access more data than necessary to demonstrate the issue. Good-faith research conducted under this section is authorized within the boundaries of the report.

## 8. Enforcement

We may investigate suspected violations, throttle or block traffic, remove content, and suspend or permanently terminate accounts. Where activity is unlawful, we may notify and cooperate with law enforcement. To appeal an enforcement action, contact `support@godisco.app`.
