# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** September 25, 2025

KingPool is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and share your information.

## 1. Information We Collect
We may collect the following types of information:
- **Personal Information**: Name, email address, phone number, and other information you provide when creating an account or interacting with our platform.
- **Usage Data**: Information about how you interact with our platform, including pages visited, features used, and time spent on the platform.
- **Search Data**: Details about your search queries across various platform contexts (e.g., home, explore, connect, profile, contest creation, dashboard, leaderboard, participants, map view, suggestions, place details, autocomplete, or other generic contexts), including keywords, search frequency, and results interacted with, to optimize your experience and improve personalized suggestions.
- **Cookies and Tracking Data**: Data collected via cookies and similar technologies for analytics, performance optimization, and enhancing user experience.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
We use the information we collect to:
- Provide, maintain, and improve our services, including platform functionality and user interface.
- Personalize your experience by tailoring content, recommendations, and suggestions based on your search history and usage patterns across various platform contexts.
- Communicate with you about your account, updates, or customer support inquiries.
- Analyze usage and search trends to enhance platform performance, optimize search functionality, and develop new features.
- Deliver targeted marketing or promotional content, where permitted by applicable law.

## 3. Data Sharing
We may share your data with:
- **Businesses**: Anonymized or aggregated data, including search data, may be shared with partners for marketing campaigns, customer engagement, or service improvements.
- **Service Providers**: Third parties that assist with platform operations, such as analytics, hosting, or customer support, under strict confidentiality agreements.
- **Legal Authorities**: When required by law, to comply with legal processes, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of KingPool, its users, or the public.

## 4. Data Retention
We retain your information, including search data, only for as long as necessary to provide our services, fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, or comply with legal obligations. Search data is typically retained for up to 12 months for personalization and analytics purposes, after which it is anonymized or deleted unless required by law.

## 5. Your Rights
You have the right to:
- Access the information we hold about you, including details of your search history.
- Request corrections to inaccurate or incomplete information.
- Request deletion of your data, including search data, subject to legal or contractual restrictions.
- Opt out of personalized suggestions or targeted marketing based on search data, where applicable, by adjusting your account settings or contacting us.
- Request a copy of your data in a portable format, where technically feasible.

## 6. Security
We implement industry-standard measures, such as encryption and access controls, to protect your data, including search data. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Changes to Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

## 8. Contact Us
If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us at privacy@kingpool.app.
